CyberGuard AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

← Back to Privacy Center

1. Overview

CyberGuard AI helps users identify potentially malicious or phishing URLs and webpages. It can also analyze supported email content for phishing indicators.

2. Information processed

  • URLs: URLs may be analyzed for phishing and malicious-website indicators.
  • Webpage information: Relevant webpage signals may be processed for security analysis.
  • Email content: When the supported email-analysis feature is used, subject, body, sender and reply-to information may be processed.
  • Extension data: Security results and related state may be stored in browser-local storage.

3. Use of information

Information is used solely for phishing detection, webpage security analysis, warnings and related extension functionality. It is not used for advertising or unrelated profiling.

4. Local service

The current architecture can communicate with a locally running service at 127.0.0.1:8000 to perform ML-based analysis.

5. Third-party threat intelligence

Optional security-analysis functionality may use third-party threat-intelligence services. If enabled, a relevant URL or security indicator may be transmitted to that provider for a security result. Third parties follow their own privacy policies.

6. Retention

CyberGuard AI is designed to minimize retention. Browser-local results and settings may remain until removed by the extension, browser or user. The local service may process request data only as needed to return a prediction unless separately configured.

7. Sharing and sale

CyberGuard AI does not sell user data or transfer it for advertising or unrelated purposes. Optional third-party threat intelligence is used for the stated security purpose.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit data handling to required functionality. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when functionality or data practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information provided on the extension's official project or store listing.
