CyberGuard AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CyberGuard AI is a browser security extension designed to help users identify potentially phishing or harmful webpages and URLs. This privacy policy explains what information the extension processes and how that information is used.

1. Information CyberGuard AI Processes

CyberGuard AI may process information necessary to perform its security analysis, including:

  • URLs and web addresses of pages being analyzed.
  • Relevant webpage information used by the extension's phishing-analysis functionality.
  • Email content from supported webmail pages when the user uses the email phishing-analysis feature.
  • Security-analysis results associated with the current browser tab.

2. How Information Is Used

This information is processed solely to provide CyberGuard AI's phishing detection and security-warning functionality. The information is used to analyze potential threats and return a security assessment to the user.

CyberGuard AI does not use this information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or unrelated marketing purposes.

3. Local CyberGuard Service

The browser extension communicates with the CyberGuard AI service running locally on the user's computer. The extension uses the local service to perform security analysis and receive analysis results.

The extension's local service is normally accessed through 127.0.0.1:8000. Communication with this local service is required for the extension's core security functionality.

4. Storage

The extension may use browser extension storage to maintain security analysis results and extension state associated with browser tabs. This information supports the operation of the extension and its user interface.

5. Notifications

CyberGuard AI may display browser notifications when its security analysis identifies a potentially harmful webpage. Notifications are used only for security-related warnings and are not used for advertising or promotional communications.

6. Third-Party Services

CyberGuard AI is designed to perform its primary analysis through the CyberGuard AI service. If an optional third-party threat-intelligence integration is enabled by the user, information may be sent to that third-party service as required to perform the requested security analysis. Users should review the applicable third party's privacy policy before enabling such integrations.

7. Data Retention

CyberGuard AI does not intend to retain browsing or email information for advertising or profiling purposes. Extension state and security results may be stored as necessary for the extension to operate and are removed when no longer required by the extension's tab-specific functionality.

8. Data Sharing

CyberGuard AI does not sell user information. Information processed by the extension is not shared for advertising or behavioral profiling. Where an optional third-party security or threat-intelligence service is used, the information necessary for that requested security analysis may be transmitted to that service.

9. Security

CyberGuard AI is intended to process information for security analysis and uses a locally running CyberGuard AI service for its primary analysis workflow. Users should keep the extension and its associated CyberGuard software up to date.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when CyberGuard AI's functionality or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions about CyberGuard AI, please contact the developer through the contact information provided with the CyberGuard AI project or its Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing.
