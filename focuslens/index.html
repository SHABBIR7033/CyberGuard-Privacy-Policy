FocusLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

← Back to Privacy Center

1. Overview

FocusLens is an ML-powered browser extension designed to help users maintain focus during self-directed study sessions. It can analyze browsing behavior and webpage or video content to estimate whether activity relates to the user's study goal and, when enabled, restrict distracting content.

2. Information processed

  • Study settings: subject, topic, study duration and selected enforcement mode.
  • Browsing activity: navigation events, tab/session activity, timing, domain information and interaction patterns used for focus features.
  • Page/video information: URLs, titles, descriptions, visible text and available metadata may be analyzed for content classification.
  • Focus statistics: locally calculated focus score, study time, distraction time and blocked-content counts.

3. Use of information

Information is used to provide Study Mode, estimate focus/distraction, classify content, determine study relevance and enforce selected focus settings. It is not intended for advertising or unrelated profiling.

4. Local processing and storage

FocusLens is designed with a privacy-first architecture. Where technically practical, behavioral data, study settings and focus statistics are processed and stored locally in the browser. Core functionality is designed not to require a central browsing-history database.

5. Sharing

FocusLens does not sell user data or share browsing behavior with advertisers. If a future version introduces an external service that requires transmitting data, this policy will be updated before that functionality is used.

6. User control

Users can stop Study Mode and manage the extension through its settings. Browser storage can also be cleared through browser extension/site-data controls.

7. Retention

Locally stored session information and statistics remain until the extension removes them, the user clears them, or storage is otherwise reset. FocusLens is designed to minimize retention.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit collection and processing to necessary functionality. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when FocusLens adds or changes features or data practices.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information provided on the extension's official project or store listing.
